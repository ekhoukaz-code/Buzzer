🏀 How to play

Guess the Player: Pick a player from the roster. Compare their team, position, height, and age with the mystery player. Use hints if you get stuck.

NBA Wordle: Guess the five-letter basketball word in six tries. Green letters are in the right place, yellow letters belong somewhere else, and grey letters are not in the word.

Your stats: Your stats and daily progress are stored in this browser. Clearing browser data can erase them.

Roster note: The game loads data from a community-maintained roster. It may not match every current NBA signing or roster move.
